          The contract, frozen

          One JSON record per query is the only thing both halves agree on. Any change bumps the version and the mock generator in the same commit.

          Distinctions the design refuses to blur

          Natural vs constructed
          Tier 1 cases are mined from genuinely separate documents. Tier 2 splits one document’s rule and exception in two. They are never blended in a headline number.
          Refinement vs opposed
          An exception narrows a rule without contesting it. An opposed pair overlaps without nesting and disagrees on the overlap. Only the second is a contradiction.
          Applicability vs outcome
          Whether scopes overlap and whether outcomes agree are decided independently, then combined. Neither alone yields the right relation.
          Preservation vs correctness
          An answer can match its reference and still drop a valid branch. The metric suite scores branches, not sentences.
          The decisive baseline
          A strong long-context model, given every passage and asked for structured branches. If it matches the pipeline, the architectural claim is falsified.

          09 The honest frontier

          The hypothesis must be
          allowed to fail.

          The instrument is built and tested. The measurement that could falsify the central claim has run once, at small scale — and it favours the baseline. That is reported here as prominently as a positive result would be.
