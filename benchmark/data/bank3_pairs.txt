# Financial-terms Tier-1 candidates, batch 3 — 2026-09-25.
#
# Monzo, Starling and Santander. The shape that worked in batch 2: a product or
# plan's TERMS against a separate FEE INFORMATION document. Banks are required
# to publish a standalone fee information document, which is what makes this
# structure reliably cross-document in a way gov.uk guides are not.
#
# Monzo in particular pairs many plan-specific terms against ONE shared fee
# schedule. That repetition is deliberate on their side and useful here: the
# general rule sits in the shared schedule and the carve-out in the plan.
#
# Several Starling entries are PDFs. fetch_web handles those, and robots.txt is
# checked per host before any request.
