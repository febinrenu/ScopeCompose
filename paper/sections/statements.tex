This study uses only publicly available research datasets, ConditionalQA and RAMDocs, under their licences, together with the authors' own previously annotated corpus. It involves no human participants, collects no personal data and required no new annotation. The guidance texts are public UK government web pages. Answers produced by the systems studied here concern eligibility for public services and must not be used as legal or official advice; this applies equally to the benchmark's own answers, which its authors state were not verified by legal professionals.
